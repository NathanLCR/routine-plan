thesis

Research questions

4 RQs
RQ1 EMPIRICAL

Measure cross-lingual judge bias magnitude via controlled perturbations.

PT-BR-only bias magnitude (D-9); English subset is a stretch goal only. Perturbation precedents: ReEval (primary), FaithEval typology. Judge-bias grounding: Zheng et al. (MT-Bench) — position/verbosity/self-enhancement. Risk-paper noise addressed via manual validation of a perturbation sample.

RQ2 EMPIRICAL

Test whether rubric decomposition improves inter-judge agreement on PT-BR RAG faithfulness.

Prompt-richness confound now controlled by the three-condition design (D-1): C1 bare-holistic / C2 elaborated-holistic (content-matched) / C3 decomposed. Zhang (arXiv:2603.28005) is the prompt-controlled correctness study grounding the confound.

RQ3 CONCEPTUAL

Critically examine whether agreement gains constitute accuracy gains.

Conceptual/critical, not empirical — no PT-BR gold labels. MEMERAG AC1 ≈ 0.45 with trained annotators is the cited ground for rejecting a self-annotated anchor (figure still to be located in-paper, body-check #2). Wallat (correctness ≠ faithfulness) is the conceptual sibling.

RQ4 DISCUSSION

Folded into Discussion (D-2, 14 Jun); no longer a standalone empirical RQ. BERTScore descriptive-only.
